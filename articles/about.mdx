**Buka Knowledge** is a medium for binding knowledge, notes, concerns into writing that is simple, easy but still meaningful. We hope this can serve as inspiration and something to think about.

This is a collaboration between **humans** and **artificial intelligence** to meet the need for practical knowledge.

Personally I called this "[Guided Intelligence Knowledge](__GHOST_URL__/guided-intelligence-knowledge-the-fusion-of-human-expertise-and-artificial-intelligence-for-smarter-decisions-2/)" or "Augmented Knowledge".

**Buka Knowledge** is part of [Buka Platforms, Inc.](https://github.com/buka-platforms) and run by [Sony AK](https://sony-ak.com).
