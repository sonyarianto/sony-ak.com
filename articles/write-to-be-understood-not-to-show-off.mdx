<p>In the age of social media, many people share information with the goal of appearing <strong>smart</strong> or <strong>intellectual</strong>. But if your goal is to share knowledge, this mindset needs to change. <strong>The main goal is not to look smart, but to make people understand.</strong></p><p>When writing or speaking on social media, consider the following:</p><h3 id="1-use-simple-language">1. <strong>Use Simple Language</strong></h3><p>Avoid jargon or technical terms that only a few people understand. The more people who can understand your message, the greater the impact. <strong>Simplicity doesn’t mean shallow, but it’s actually a sign of deep understanding.</strong></p><h3 id="2-focus-on-the-message-not-the-style">2. <strong>Focus on the Message, Not the Style</strong></h3><p>Sometimes we’re tempted to write in a flowery style, full of metaphors or cool terms. But if it makes the message harder to understand, it’s better to avoid it. <strong>The beauty of writing is not measured by how complicated it is, but by how effectively it conveys the message.</strong></p><h3 id="3-be-concise-and-clear">3. <strong>Be Concise and Clear</strong></h3><p>Don’t beat around the bush. People’s time is limited. <strong>If you can convey your message in 10 words, don’t use 20.</strong> Make every word work hard to get your point across.</p><h3 id="4-use-examples-or-analogies">4. <strong>Use Examples or Analogies</strong></h3><p>People find it easier to understand something when there’s a real example or simple analogy. For example, if you want to explain the concept of an “algorithm,” it's better to use an analogy like <strong>“an algorithm is like a recipe; there are specific steps to follow to achieve a result.”</strong></p><h3 id="5-consider-your-audience">5. <strong>Consider Your Audience</strong></h3><p>Who will be reading your post? Are they beginners or experienced? <strong>Adjust your language and the depth of information</strong> so they can easily understand. Don’t let your message only be understood by a few people.</p><h3 id="6-encourage-discussion">6. <strong>Encourage Discussion</strong></h3><p>Don’t be afraid if your post seems too simple. In fact, by making things simple, you open up space for people to ask questions and engage in discussions. <strong>Interaction is more valuable than just impressing others.</strong></p><h3 id="7-the-importance-of-a-clear-structure">7. <strong>The Importance of a Clear Structure</strong></h3><p>Use headings, bullet points, or well-structured paragraphs. <strong>The reader’s eyes should be able to quickly grasp the essence of the post without reading everything from start to finish.</strong></p><h3 id="8-make-people-remember-not-just-read">8. <strong>Make People Remember, Not Just Read</strong></h3><p>A good post is one that people remember even after they close the screen. <strong>Use storytelling or impactful facts</strong> so that your message stays with them longer.</p><h3 id="finally">Finally</h3><p>When sharing on social media, <strong>the impact doesn’t come from the most complex post, but from the one that’s easiest to understand.</strong> Simplify, focus, and make sure your message is accessible to as many people as possible.</p><p>So, has your post today been clear and easy to understand? 😊</p><h3 id="support-us">Support Us</h3>
