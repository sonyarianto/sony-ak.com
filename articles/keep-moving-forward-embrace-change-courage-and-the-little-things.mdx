Life is an unpredictable journey, full of twists, turns, and moments of uncertainty. It’s easy to get stuck in the loop of regrets over what we can’t change or distracted by things that don’t add value to our lives. But what if we shift our focus? What if we choose to embrace change, courage, and the little things that make life worth living?

Here’s how you can start, along with some important considerations to keep in mind.

### **Let Go of What You Can't Control**

First and foremost, stop dwelling on things that are out of your hands. **You can’t rewrite the past, but you can create a better future.** Mute that noisy WhatsApp group, unfollow accounts that make you feel less, and clear your digital and mental clutter. Your energy is finite—don’t waste it on things or people that don’t uplift you.

### **Organize, Plan, and Execute**

Use tools like a calendar or a to-do list—not just to write things down but to actually do them. **Small actions build momentum.** Even completing one small task can give you a sense of accomplishment. Don’t aim for perfection; aim for progress.

### **Feed Your Mind and Soul**

Engage with content that makes you think. **Read books, articles, or even comics that inspire or teach you something.** It doesn’t always have to be heavy—sometimes light, entertaining reads can offer surprising insights. Watch movies that resonate with you, even if they don’t have stellar reviews. Art doesn’t have to be popular to be meaningful.

Take time to **eat without distractions.** Savor the moment. Appreciate the flavors. Something as simple as a good meal can ground you in the present and remind you to enjoy life’s small pleasures.

### **Step Out of Your Comfort Zone**

Don’t wait for the “right” moment—**you’ll never feel 100% ready.** Start anyway. Whether it’s taking a morning walk, trying a new hobby, or reaching out to an old friend, courage often begins with small, shaky steps. Yes, it might feel awkward or even scary, but that’s how you grow.

-   **Take initiative:** Go to the park alone or explore a nearby market. Start conversations with strangers—like the gorengan vendor or a parking attendant. These little interactions remind you that you’re part of a larger community.
-   **Try new things:** Join a sports club, learn an instrument, or attend a local workshop. Growth doesn’t happen in comfort; it happens in the unfamiliar.
-   **Be okay with imperfection:** Whether it’s a project, a relationship, or a workout, don’t let the fear of "meh" results stop you. **Done is better than perfect.**

### **Detach From Your Devices**

In a world that’s always online, **disconnecting for even an hour can do wonders.** Step outside without your phone. Pay attention to the world around you. Smile at someone, pet a stray cat, or simply enjoy the fresh air. These seemingly insignificant moments can restore a sense of calm and presence.

### **Focus on Your Inner Growth**

Life isn’t a race to be better than others—it’s about becoming a better version of yourself. Stop comparing and stop criticizing, especially yourself.

-   **Surround yourself with positivity:** Spend time with people who inspire you, not drain you.
-   **Learn from others:** Not to compare, but to remind yourself that **if they can, so can you.**
-   **Celebrate small wins:** Every step forward, no matter how tiny, is progress.

### **Face Rejection and Failure Head-On**

What’s the worst that can happen? A no? A mistake? **These are just steps on the path to success.** Laugh at your blunders, learn from them, and keep moving. Every failure is proof that you’re trying.

### **Make Time for Play and Creativity**

Life isn’t just about responsibilities. **Give yourself permission to have fun and create.** Paint, write, cook, or build something, even if it’s just for you. Let your imagination run wild. Play like a child again—there’s no shame in finding joy in the simplest things.

### **Stay Present and Grateful**

The biggest gift you can give yourself is presence. Worrying about the future or overthinking the past steals the beauty of now.

-   **Pause and reflect:** What’s one good thing that happened today? It could be as simple as a delicious sambal or a stranger’s smile.
-   **Express gratitude:** Thank people for the little things, whether it’s a friend’s kind words or a cashier’s patience.

### **Dare to Take the First Step**

Courage isn’t the absence of fear; it’s moving forward despite it. Whether it’s trying something new, talking to someone you admire, or pursuing a dream that scares you, **often, all it takes is one brave moment to set everything in motion.**

### **Finally**

The world doesn’t need you to be perfect—it needs you to keep going. **Life is about progress, not perfection.** Embrace change, honor your journey, and cherish the little things. Take that first step, no matter how small, and remember: every step forward is a victory in itself.

You’ve got this. Keep moving forward.
