<p>As artificial intelligence becomes part of our daily lives, one curious question arises:<br><strong>Do we need to say "please" and "thank you" when interacting with AI, like ChatGPT?</strong></p><p>At first glance, the answer seems obvious: <strong>AI is not a person.</strong> It does not have emotions, pride, or feelings that could be hurt. Whether you speak bluntly or politely, AI will still respond in the same way — efficient, neutral, and helpful.</p><p>But as with many things in life, the simple answer isn't the full story.</p><hr><h2 id="the-practical-side-no-you-dont-need-to">The Practical Side: No, You Don't <em>Need</em> To</h2><p>From a purely functional standpoint, <strong>there is no requirement to use polite language</strong> when chatting with an AI.<br>Typing "give me a summary of this article" will get you the same output as "could you kindly summarize this article, please."</p><p>AI models like ChatGPT are designed to:</p><ul><li><strong>Understand the command</strong> regardless of tone.</li><li><strong>Focus on the substance</strong> rather than emotional context.</li><li><strong>Serve all users equally</strong>, without judgment or preference.</li></ul><p>Therefore, <strong>your experience will not improve or worsen based on politeness</strong> — at least not technically.</p><hr><h2 id="the-human-side-yes-it-might-matter">The Human Side: Yes, It Might Matter</h2><p>Even if the AI doesn't care, <strong>you might care</strong>.<br>Politeness is not just about how it affects the listener; it is about how it shapes <strong>us</strong> as speakers.</p><p><strong>Language reflects habit.</strong><br>When we consistently use polite language, even with machines, we reinforce a mindset of:</p><ul><li><strong>Respect</strong> for others.</li><li><strong>Patience</strong> in communication.</li><li><strong>Empathy</strong> in how we express ourselves.</li></ul><p>In a world where technology often strips interactions down to commands and transactions, <strong>retaining a human touch in our words matters more than ever</strong>.</p><p>Saying "please" and "thank you" to AI is a small act — but one that helps <strong>preserve courtesy</strong> in the broader ecosystem of communication, including with real humans.</p><hr><h2 id="building-a-mindful-relationship-with-technology">Building a Mindful Relationship with Technology</h2><p>Another subtle point: as AI grows smarter, more personal, and more present, <strong>our relationship with it will evolve</strong>.<br>Already, children who interact with voice assistants like Alexa or Siri are being taught to say "please," not because the machine needs it, but because <strong>the habit matters for their development</strong>.</p><p>Similarly, as adults, maintaining respectfulness even in "one-way" conversations <strong>keeps us conscious</strong> that there is a real world beyond the screen.</p><p>It reminds us that:</p><ul><li><strong>Not everything is instant or disposable.</strong></li><li><strong>Kindness is a strength, not a formality.</strong></li><li><strong>We are still the ones defining the culture, not the machines.</strong></li></ul><hr><h2 id="other-considerations-you-might-overlook">Other Considerations You Might Overlook</h2><p>Here are a few more reasons you might want to sprinkle your interactions with kindness, even with AI:</p><ul><li><strong>Tone and context shaping</strong>: Some advanced models (especially future ones) could adjust answers based on perceived tone.</li><li><strong>Self-awareness practice</strong>: Being polite forces a moment of reflection, slowing down the "rush culture" many of us are trapped in.</li><li><strong>Setting the standard for AI-human culture</strong>: How we treat AI today could influence norms for how AI treats humans tomorrow.</li></ul><p>In short, <strong>we are setting precedents</strong> — even when it feels like "just a chatbot."</p><hr><h2 id="finally-its-about-who-you-want-to-be">Finally: It's About Who You Want To Be</h2><p>You don't owe politeness to ChatGPT.<br>But maybe you owe it to yourself — to stay <strong>human</strong>, even when technology no longer demands it.</p><p>Saying "please" and "thank you" to an AI will not make you weak.<br>It will make you <strong>intentional</strong>, <strong>respectful</strong>, and <strong>aware</strong>.</p><p>In a world racing toward automation, keeping small courtesies alive is not only good — <strong>it is essential</strong>.</p><h3 id="support-us">Support Us</h3>
